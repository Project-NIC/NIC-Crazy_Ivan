## On the header

She sits on the Raspberry Pi's header; with a ROCK 5T there is no Kormilica, the ROCK's
Umnicas and eFuse are on [Terem](../terem/README.md) under it.

- **Stacked:** Kormilica sits on the Raspberry Pi's 40-pin header with a stacking header, and
  Mamka sits on top of her. Of the 40 pins Kormilica takes only the 5 V pins 2 and 4, which she
  feeds, and the eight grounds (6, 9, 14, 20, 25, 30, 34, 39); the rest pass straight up to
  Mamka.
- **A strip and a wing,** like Mamka: over the Raspberry Pi only the strip with the header; the
  converters and Umnica's M.2 socket reach out past its edge on the wing, beside Mamka's, in the
  backpack's air channel (see [Babuška](../babuska/CONSTRUCTION.md#the-backpack)). Nothing lies over
  the Active Cooler.
- **Cables:** the battery comes in from Babuška; EN and PGOOD go to Mamka on a short cable, so
  Mamka switches the Raspberry Pi as before.
