# Names

**Why the backpack is a family.**

↑ [NIC-Crazy_Ivan](README.md)

</div>

---

The names are a family joke and nothing more. It started with Mamka, Russian slang for a
motherboard; the rest of the backpack and the suit became her family, in Russian words written in
Latin letters the Czech way.

The project's name and the picture on the front page are a joke too, nothing more.

| name | meaning | why |
|---|---|---|
| [**Mamka**](mamka/README.md) | mum | Russian slang for a motherboard; she runs the suit and feeds her own children, the Rebjata. |
| [**Baťa**](bata/README.md) | dad, and in slang the boss | the computer: he does the thinking for the family. |
| [**Umnica**](bata/HARDWARE.md#umnica) | the clever one | the Hailo-10H, the clever daughter who thinks for dad. |
| [**Kormilica**](kormilica/README.md) | the wet nurse | she feeds other people's children: the computers and Umnica, not Mamka's own. |
| [**Terem**](terem/README.md) | the upper chamber, where the daughters lived | the board under the ROCK 5T that holds the two Umnicas, each with her own supply, and feeds the ROCK itself. |
| [**Babuška**](babuska/README.md) | grandma | the battery: grandma keeps the pantry, and everyone eats from her. |
| [**Ključnica**](babuska/HARDWARE.md#the-bms) | the housekeeper with the keys | the BMS: she holds the keys to Babuška's pantry and decides what goes in and out. |
| [**Deduška**](bata/HARDWARE.md#deduška) | grandpa | Babuška's husband; the old man has the big computer. |
| [**Ďaďa Sem**](bata/HARDWARE.md#ďaďa-sem) | Uncle Sam | the uncle from America, who watches everything: the cameras. |
| [**Budilnik**](mamka/HARDWARE.md#budilnik) | the alarm clock | the start button and its latch: one press wakes the household, and when everyone has shut down it lets the house go dark. |
| [**Storož**](bata/SOFTWARE.md#storož) | the night watchman | Baťa's halt daemon: when something fatal breaks he puts the household's services out, leaves the gate open, the terminal and the link to Deduška, and says why. Our Tron, the program that fights for the users. |
| **Kommunalka** | a communal flat | the backpack, where the whole family lives and shares one kitchen. |
| [**Chodiki**](mamka/HARDWARE.md#clock) | the wall clock with weights | the suit's clock: the whole household lives by it. |
| [**Rubaška**](rubaska/README.md) | the shirt | the suit. In Russian, as in Czech, a child born in a shirt is born lucky: the "shirt" is the caul, a second skin, and so is the suit. |
| [**Rebjata**](rubaska/HARDWARE.md#sensing-module) | the kids | the sensing modules: Mamka's own children. |
| [**Pupovina**](porjadok/HARDWARE.md#links-between-boards) | the umbilical cord | the lines from Mamka to the Rebjata: power, the clock and data. |
| [**Vnučata**](rubaska/HARDWARE.md#the-glove-and-the-foot) | the grandchildren | the small processors in the glove and the foot: they hang on one of Mamka's children, not on Mamka. |
| [**Porjadok**](porjadok/README.md) | order | the household's rules, which every member keeps: processor, firmware, links, output. |
| [**Pitanije**](porjadok/HARDWARE.md#power) | food, and a power supply | the suit's power, from Babuška down to every module; the Russian word means both. |

Nataša, the unit in the headband, and the assistants Soňa, Míša, Taťána and Nikita are older and
have names of their own (see [the README](README.md#assistants)).
