## On the head

- **The microphones,** one on each side of the head, for stereo. Their ports are at the bottom, so
  the sound comes through a hole in the board under each one; what covers the holes against sweat
  and wind is still open.
- **The motors,** one on each side: left, right, the forehead and the nape. They sit as far from
  the microphones as the headband allows and are held apart mechanically.
- **The buttons,** four at each temple or close by, so that a blind wearer finds them by where
  they sit; a cable of four lines and ground to each temple.
- **Míša** plugs into one socket on the forehead side.
- **The board** lies flat in the headband: its polymers are 1.4–1.8 mm tall, where NIC-Heimdall's
  50 V hybrid polymer stands 10.2 mm.
- **The headphones** are any wired pair in the 3.5 mm jack.

## Open questions

- where the microphones sit on the headband, and what covers their holes against sweat and
  wind,
- the motors against the microphones: as far apart as the headband allows, and held apart
  mechanically; Soňa knows when the motors run and can leave that time out.
