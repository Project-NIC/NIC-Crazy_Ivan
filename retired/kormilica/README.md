*Kormilica* is Russian for a wet nurse, the woman who feeds other people's children.
[Mamka](../../mamka/README.md) feeds her own, the suit's modules; Kormilica feeds a Raspberry Pi
and its Umnica (see [Baťa](../../bata/README.md)). A ROCK 5T needs her not: it and its Umnicas eat
from [Terem](../../terem/README.md).
