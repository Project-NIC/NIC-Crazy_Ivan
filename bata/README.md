# Baťa

**The computer in the backpack:** a Radxa ROCK 5T, with Umnica, the Hailo-10H, or with its own
NPU alone. He reads the suit's data from Mamka, runs the model and gives the output.

↑ [NIC-Crazy_Ivan](../README.md)

</div>

---

*Baťa* is Russian for dad, and in slang for the boss. [Mamka](../mamka/README.md) runs
the suit; Baťa does the thinking, or rather his clever daughter Umnica does, or in the small
fitting his own NPU. When a second computer is wanted beside him, that is Deduška, grandpa.

| | what | which computer |
|---|---|---|
| **A: Baťa** | the suit computer, with Mamka on his header | a Radxa ROCK 5T |
| **B: Deduška** | a full computer beside him, if wanted | a ROCK 5T with an NVMe SSD, over Ethernet |
| **Ďaďa Sem** | the cameras, into Baťa | two USB cameras |
